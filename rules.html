Local Rules

Play it as it lies. Here's exactly how.

House rules

1

No Mulligans

No do-overs. Whatever you hit, counts, even the shanks.

2

No Gimmies. Always Hole Out

Every putt gets holed. During the Stableford qualifying rounds, normal Stableford etiquette applies: once you can't score any points on a hole, pick up and move to the next tee. In the matchplay finals, where every stroke counts, hole out everything.

3

No Ball Re-Placement

Play the ball as it lies: on the fairway, in a divot, behind a tree, wherever it ends up. The only exception is when a club's own local rules specifically allow placement (for example winter "preferred lies"); check with the club before assuming it applies.

4

Out of Bounds

Stroke and distance: play again from the same spot, with a 1-stroke penalty. Example: OB off the tee → your next shot is your 3rd.

If you're not sure whether your ball is OB, play a provisional ball before walking forward (see "Provisional Ball" below).

If you didn't play a provisional: find where the ball crossed the OB line and drop anywhere between that point and two club-lengths into the fairway (not nearer the hole), with a 2-stroke penalty. Example: OB off the tee → next shot is your 4th.

5

Unplayable Ball in the Rough

Three options, each with a 1-stroke penalty:

  • Stroke and distance: go back and play again from your previous spot.
  • Back-on-the-line: drop anywhere on a straight line running from the hole through where your ball lay, as far back as you like.
  • Two club-lengths: drop within two club-lengths of the unplayable spot, not nearer the hole. If that's still bad rough, tough luck: you can't instead drop into the fairway unless the fairway itself falls within that two-club-length radius.

Play it fair: Gents, we understand that you want to be friendly and understanding towards your flight mates, but for fairness in the Kangaroo Cup, please just follow the rules, so no-one has an advantage over someone else in another flight.

Good to know

  • ●Lost ball: same penalty as OB (stroke and distance, 1 stroke). You get 3 minutes to search before it's officially lost.
  • ●Provisional ball: must be clearly announced ("that's a provisional") before you hit it; otherwise it's treated as your ball in play, even if the original is later found safe.
  • ●Penalty areas (red/yellow stakes): unlike in a bunker, you're allowed to ground your club and move loose impediments here.
  • ●Accidental ball movement: if you or your gear accidentally nudge your ball (searching for it, lining up, etc.), that's a 1-stroke penalty and it goes back to its original spot, not played from where it rolled to.
  • ●For those with real hang-overs: a stroke is defined as the forward movement of the club made to strike the ball (it's the intent that matters), even if the ball doesn't move ;)
