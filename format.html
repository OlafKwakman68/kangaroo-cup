Kangaroo Cup Format

Simple, fair, and fun finals format

1

Friday & Saturday: Qualifying

Stableford with handicap correction. Total scores across both rounds set the Sunday draw.

Player A Player B Player C Player D Player E Player F Player G Player H

1st–4th → The Final

Player A Player B Player C Player D

5th–8th → Losers Flight

Player E Player F Player G Player H
⚖️

Handicap correction: this only kicks in when a player scores more than 36 Stableford points in a qualifying round. Score 36 or fewer and nothing changes — your handicap carries over as-is. Score more than 36 and your handicap for the next day drops by the number of points above 36.

  • Plays off 25.0, scores 38 points (2 over 36) → next day starts off 23.0
  • Plays off 32.4, scores 29 points (under 36) → next day stays at 32.4
2

Sunday: The Final (1st–4th)

Match play with handicap correction, split across the two nines. The front nine decides who's playing for what on the back nine.

Front nine

Player A (1st) VS Player D (4th)
Player B (2nd) VS Player C (3rd)

Back nine

Front-nine winners VS each other
plays for the Cup
Front-nine losers VS each other
plays for 3rd & 4th
3

Sunday: Losers Flight (5th–8th)

Same match play format as the Final, split across the two nines, seeded by qualifying position within the flight.

Front nine

Player E (5th) VS Player H (8th)
Player F (6th) VS Player G (7th)

Back nine

Front-nine winners VS each other
plays for 5th
Front-nine losers VS each other
plays for 7th
4

Scoring the Final

Each nine-hole match is straight match play — most holes won after nine takes it. Front-nine winners advance to play for the Cup over the back nine; front-nine losers play off for 3rd and 4th over the back nine.

5

Scoring the Losers Flight

Same scoring as the Final: front-nine winners advance to play for 5th over the back nine; front-nine losers play off for 7th over the back nine.

6

Tiebreak

  • 1Front nine tied (Final or Losers Flight) → higher starting qualifying position goes through
  • 2Back nine tied after 18 holes (Final or Losers Flight) → sudden-death playoff, format and venue decided on the day by the tournament director
💡

Why this works: no player is forced to beat the best score of both opponents on every hole, and splitting both flights across the two nines means the back nine is always playing for something real — the Cup and 3rd/4th in the Final, 5th and 7th in the Losers Flight. Both flights use the same fair, seeded bracket format.
