Kangaroo Cup Format

Simple, fair, and fun finals format

1

Friday & Saturday: Qualifying

Stableford with handicap correction. Total scores across both rounds decide who plays in which Sunday flight.

Player A Player B Player C Player D Player E Player F Player G

1st–3rd →

Player A Player B Player C

4th–6th →

Player D Player E Player F

The 7th-placed qualifier finishes 7th outright — no playoff needed for a field of one.

⚖️

Handicap correction: this only kicks in when a player scores more than 36 Stableford points in a qualifying round. Score 36 or fewer and nothing changes — your handicap carries over as-is. Score more than 36 and your handicap for the next day drops by the number of points above 36.

  • Plays off 25.0, scores 38 points (2 over 36) → next day starts off 23.0
  • Plays off 32.4, scores 29 points (under 36) → next day stays at 32.4
2

Sunday: Winners Flight (1st–3rd)

Three-ball match play. Each finalist plays a separate match against each of the other two, all at once.

Player A VS Player B
Player A VS Player C
Player B VS Player C
3

Sunday: Minor Flight (4th–6th)

Same format, one group down. Three-ball match play among the next-best qualifiers decides 4th, 5th and 6th place.

Player D VS Player E
Player D VS Player F
Player E VS Player F
4

Scoring

In each flight, every player plays 2 individual matches, one against each of the other two.

Win2 points
Halved match1 point
Loss0 points

Points across both matches are added up; highest total after 18 holes wins the flight. In the Winners Flight, that's the Kangaroo Cup.

5

Tiebreak

  • 1Head-to-head result
  • 2Better Friday/Saturday qualifying position
💡

Why this works: no player is forced to beat the best score of both opponents on every hole. Each match is decided on its own, so it stays a fair, simple final format for mixed handicaps — and running two flights means every player still has something real to play for on Sunday.
