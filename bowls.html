Lawn Bowls

Friday 16 October, 4pm · Sorrento Bowling Club

How it works

  • 9 players, 4 lanes, one-on-one. Four matches run at the same time, so one player waits each round and fetches the beers.
  • Each match is 3 ends. Everyone rolls 4 bowls per end. You score 1 shot for every bowl that is closer to the jack than your opponent's nearest (max 4 per end).
  • Win = 2 points, draw = 1, loss = 0. Shot difference breaks ties.
  • Round 1 is pre-drawn. Rounds 2 to 4 pair players by the standings (1st v 2nd, 3rd v 4th and so on), no rematches. The lowest-ranked player who hasn't waited yet fetches the beers.
  • Nobody starts the next round until every match is finished.
  • After Round 4 the top 2 play the final on their own (3 ends, one sudden-death end if it's a draw). Everyone else watches.
